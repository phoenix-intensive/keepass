Our History

KeePass Password Safe was first released in November 2003 by Dominik Reichl, who continues to lead the project to this day. The project was born out of the need for a secure, open-source password management solution that would give users complete control over their sensitive data.

The first stable version, KeePass 1.0, was released in 2004 and quickly gained popularity among security-conscious users. In 2008, KeePass 2.0 was introduced with a completely rewritten codebase, offering enhanced features and improved security.

Our Technology

KeePass is built on a foundation of proven security technologies and best practices. Our approach to security is comprehensive, addressing not just data encryption but also memory protection, key derivation, and protection against various attack vectors.

Core Security Features:

  • AES-256 Encryption

    We use the Advanced Encryption Standard with 256-bit keys, approved by the NSA for top-secret information.

  • ChaCha20 Cipher

    An alternative to AES that provides excellent performance on systems without AES hardware acceleration.

  • Argon2 Key Derivation

    The winner of the Password Hashing Competition, designed to be resistant to GPU cracking attacks.

  • Memory Protection

    Sensitive data in memory is encrypted to prevent cold boot attacks and memory dumps.

  • Anti-Keylogging Measures

    Two-channel auto-type obfuscation helps protect against keyloggers when entering credentials.
