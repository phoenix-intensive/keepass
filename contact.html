Frequently Asked Questions

Is KeePass really free?

+

Yes, KeePass is completely free and open-source software. There are no premium features, no subscriptions, and no hidden costs. It's developed by volunteers and supported by donations.

How secure is KeePass?

+

KeePass uses AES-256 encryption, which is considered military-grade and is practically unbreakable with current technology. As long as you use a strong master password, your data is extremely secure.

Can I use KeePass on my phone?

+

While KeePass itself is designed for Windows, there are several unofficial ports available for Android (like KeePassDX and KeePass2Android) and iOS (like KeePassium and Strongbox).

What happens if I forget my master password?

+

If you forget your master password, there is no way to recover your database. This is a security feature, not a bug. It's recommended to create a backup of your database and store it securely, or use a key file in addition to your master password.

Can I sync my passwords across devices?

+

Yes, you can store your KeePass database file in a cloud storage service like Dropbox, Google Drive, or OneDrive to sync it across devices. The database remains encrypted, so even if someone gains access to your cloud storage, they can't access your passwords without your master password.

Is KeePass compatible with browser extensions?

+

Yes, there are several browser extensions that work with KeePass, such as KeePassXC-Browser, KeePassHttp, and chromeIPass. These extensions allow you to auto-fill login forms directly from your KeePass database.

Can I import passwords from other password managers?

+

Yes, KeePass supports importing passwords from many other password managers and formats, including LastPass, 1Password, Dashlane, and browser password exports. You can find import plugins and detailed instructions on the official KeePass website.

How often should I update KeePass?

+

It's recommended to keep KeePass updated to the latest version to ensure you have the most recent security patches and features. However, KeePass is very stable, and older versions remain secure as long as they're using strong encryption.
