Key Advantages of KeePass

Complete Privacy

Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to sync it. No company has access to your passwords, and there's no risk of server breaches.

Open Source Security

The code is fully transparent and reviewed by security experts worldwide. No hidden backdoors or vulnerabilities can remain undetected.

Zero Cost

KeePass is completely free, with no premium tiers, subscriptions, or hidden fees. All features are available to all users.

Ultimate Flexibility

Customize KeePass to your exact needs with plugins and extensions. Store your database wherever you want - locally, in the cloud, or on a USB drive.

Portable & Offline

Use KeePass without an internet connection. Carry it on a USB drive and use it on any computer without installation.

Active Community

Benefit from a large, active community that continuously improves the software, creates plugins, and provides support.
